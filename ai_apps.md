# Module B (AI Applications on Microsoft Azure)

## Specialization Overview
### Program Scope
#### Build and modernize AI applications on Azure across all three core workloads
* _Azure Application Services and Container Services_
* _Azure Database Services_
* _Azure AI Services_

### Prerequisites
#### Active Solutions Partner designation
* _Solutions Partner for Data & AI (Azure)_
* _Solutions Partner for Digital & App Innovation (Azure)_

### Evidence Notes
#### Audit evidence window
* _Projects should be completed in the past 12 months_
* _Greenfield projects are accepted as long as they demonstrate the controls_

* _Azure AI Services including Azure AI Studio, or Azure OpenAI Service, or Azure AI Search, or Azure AI Vision, or Azure AI Speech, or Azure AI Language, or Azure Document Intelligence_
